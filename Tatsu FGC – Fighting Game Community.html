What we do

We run tournaments and casual sessions, and our purpose is to build a community in every fighting game, so players of each game have a place to meet and compete.

Tournaments

Organized brackets with posted rules and schedules, open to every skill level.

Casual play

Open setups and friendly matches. Beginners are welcome.

Every game

We grow a local scene for each fighting game, one community at a time.
